# Compatible Steward Selection Framework

**Document role:** Who is allowed to shape rooms inside Hideout — selection criteria, evidence bar, governance.  
**Status:** Canonical. Durable doctrine — not a description of any current person.  
**Date:** June 2026

**Pair with:** `HIDEOUT_HOUSE_DOCTRINE.md` (what the house is) · `ATMOSPHERE_GOVERNANCE.md` (what may be added)  
**This file answers:** Who gets to help build rooms inside it?

Matteo may pass intuitively. Future contributors must not be judged on vibes. This framework exists so **principles** govern admission, not affection or enthusiasm.

---

## The category

**Compatible steward** — not customer, employee, contractor, collaborator, tenant, or opportunist.

Someone granted **influence over a room** inside the house because they reliably strengthen the house without requiring authorship credit, territory, or platform.

---

## 1. Distinctions (behavioral patterns)

| Type | Pattern | House relationship |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Customer** | Transacts. Takes value. May love the place. | No influence over rooms. |
| **Employee** | Executes defined work for compensation. | Influence only within assigned scope — not room creation. |
| **Contractor** | Delivers a scoped deliverable. Leaves. | No ongoing room authority unless promoted through framework. |
| **Collaborator** | Works with you on a project. May seek co-authorship. | Temporary. Credit and scope negotiated per project — not house stewardship. |
| **Well-meaning enthusiast** | Cares. Offers ideas. Lacks expertise or restraint. | Often increases **inventory**. High risk until proven otherwise. |
| **Opportunist** | Uses Hideout as platform for self-expression, audience, or status. | Disqualifying. Treats house as stage, not stewardship. |
| **Compatible steward** | Cares about **outcome** not credit. Contributes before being asked. Expertise the house lacks. Adds signal without noise. Expands coherence, not complexity. | May be granted influence over a **defined room** under governance below. |

**Collaborator vs steward:** A collaborator asks *what can we build together?* A steward asks *what does the house need?*

---

## 2. Evidence before influence

No one receives room authority on promise, charisma, or shared taste alone.

**Minimum evidence bar** (typically observed over time, not one conversation):

| Evidence | What it proves |
|----------|----------------|
| Contributes before receiving | Care about outcome, not transaction |
| Improves without being asked | Stewardship instinct, not performance |
| Accepts edit without ego fracture | Compatible with Brice's editor role |
| Creates atmosphere, not inventory | Passes atmosphere filter (see `ATMOSPHERE_GOVERNANCE.md`) |
| Expands coherence, not complexity | Medici adjacency — new signal that still feels like Hideout |
| Brings non-predictable competence | Expertise Brice does not possess — not redundant authorship |

**Influence tiers** (earned, not assumed):

1. **Guest contribution** — one-time or episodic; Brice approves each addition.
2. **Room steward** — ongoing authority within one domain (garden, Sunday vinyl, floral). Brice retains veto and atmosphere gate.
3. **House advisor** — consulted on cross-room coherence; no unilateral adds.

Promotion between tiers requires **observed evidence**, not enthusiasm.

---

## 3. Predictive signals (green)

- Contributes before receiving
- Improves without being asked
- Cares about outcome, not credit
- Creates atmosphere, not inventory
- Expands coherence rather than complexity
- Takes correction as editing, not attack
- Brings expertise the house lacks
- Respects calm — does not fill silence with activity
- Productive adjacency — different world, compatible frequency
- Leaves the room better when absent (systems hold without them performing)

---

## 4. Disqualifying signals (red)

- Demands territory ("my corner," "my brand here")
- Increases noise — visual, operational, or cultural
- Seeks attention over outcome
- Creates dependency (house cannot function without their drama or approval)
- Treats Hideout as platform for self-expression rather than stewardship
- Adds inventory when atmosphere was the need
- Bypasses edit — implements before consultation when influence not yet earned
- Competes with coherence for visibility
- Well-meaning but unrestrained — enthusiasm without restraint is the most common failure mode

**One red signal does not always disqualify forever.** Repeated pattern without correction does.

## 6. Governance model

### Brice retains sole authority

- Atmosphere and calm — non-delegable
- Final veto on any addition, removal, or visible change
- Economics and hours — operating brain
- Who is promoted to steward tiers
- Revocation when inventory or noise accumulates
- Nervous-system safety of the house

### Delegated (room steward tier, when earned)

- Day-to-day decisions **within defined room** (e.g. plant placement, floral rotation, Sunday set sequence within slot rules)
- Execution within pre-agreed aesthetic and atmosphere constraints
- Bringing proposals — not implementing without approval until trust is established

### Requires consultation (not unilateral)

- Anything visible from corridor or patio — always
- New recurring programming
- Cross-room changes (affects more than one domain)
- Partnerships, pricing, signage, menu identity
- First implementation in any new domain — always pilot, then review

**Editor model:** Others expand. Brice edits. Stewards propose; Brice admits or rejects. *I love this, but it still has to feel calm* — that sentence is governance, not resistance.

---

## 7. Admission process (default)

Not a committee. Not vibes.

1. **Observe** — evidence bar signals over weeks or months, not one meeting.
2. **Pilot** — one bounded contribution. Brice edits. Measure atmosphere impact.
3. **Promote or pause** — room steward tier only if pilot strengthened house.
4. **Review** — periodic. Stewards can be demoted. Rooms can be closed.

Document pilots in operating brain experiment ledger when material.

---

## 8. What this is not

- Not an HR framework for employees
- Not a partnership legal template
- Not a personality test
- Not permanent membership — stewardship is **room-scoped** and revocable
- Not openness — selective permeability remains the law
