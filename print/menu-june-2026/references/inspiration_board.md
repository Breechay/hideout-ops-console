# Inspiration Board — Mood Directions

Generate **references**, not final designs. Reject coffee beans, latte art, hipster café aesthetics.

---

## Direction A — Private club

Luxury members club collateral, dark paper, cream typography, understated signage. References: Aman resorts stationery, Soho House member materials, private club notice boards.

**Feel:** You belong here. No one is selling you anything.

---

## Direction B — Boutique hotel

Hotel welcome packet, room-service card, quiet luxury, editorial hospitality. Dark cover, cream interior type, generous margins.

**Feel:** Thoughtful placement. Calm competence.

---

## Direction C — Museum

Modern museum wayfinding, gallery membership materials, restrained typography. Strong grid, minimal color, one accent.

**Feel:** Cultural institution, not commercial promotion.

---

## Direction D — Japanese café

Minimal Japanese coffee house printed collateral, strong negative space, premium matte paper, no decoration.

**Feel:** Precision and restraint.
