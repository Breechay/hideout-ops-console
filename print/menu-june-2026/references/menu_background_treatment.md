# Menu Background — Photoshop Treatment

Apply to `menu-background.jpg` before rebuilding column boards or exporting card templates.

---

## Target

Volcanic stone — warm, dark, low-contrast texture. Food and cream type read forward.

---

## Steps (Affinity / Photoshop)

1. Duplicate background layer
2. **Levels or Curves:** Pull midtones down ~10–15% (output black stays `#0E0C0A` family, not pure #000)
3. **Texture soften:** High-pass or clarity reduction ~10–20% on texture layer only
4. **Warm:** Photo Filter Warming 85% at 5–8% OR slight shift toward orange in shadows (+3–5 in a/b in Lab)
5. **Overlay check:** Place cream text sample (`#F5ECD7`) — must pass WCAG-style glance test at 3 feet
6. Export as `menu-background-v2.jpg` — keep v1 archived

---

## Do not

- Crush to pure black
- Remove texture entirely (stone feel is brand)
- Cool the gray (reads institutional)
- Increase texture contrast (competes with food photography)

---

## Validation

Hold printed sample next to `mockups/` physical card mockups. Same house?
