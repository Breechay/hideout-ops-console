# Layout Rules

One grid family across all seven pieces.

---

## Margins

- **Bleed:** 0.125" outside trim
- **Safe zone:** 0.125" inside trim — no text or QR outside
- **Card internal padding:** 0.25" minimum (≈18px at 300 DPI)

---

## QR placement

- Always inside safe zone
- Caramel frame = the one accent on most pieces
- Centered for tent cards and lobby stand
- Right-aligned acceptable on horizontal business card
- Never corner-stamp smaller than 1"

---

## Hierarchy (top → bottom)

1. Wordmark (small except hero pieces)
2. Headline (display italic — one thought)
3. Supporting line (DM Sans muted)
4. QR + scan instruction
5. Hairline separator (cream 10%)
6. Hours / metadata (DM Mono, 60% opacity)

---

## Photography overlay

If using AI background:
- Place on texture group below text
- Additional void multiply 50–70% so cream type reads
- Never place text directly on busy photo without overlay

---

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Same background treatment as other pieces in batch
- [ ] Only one caramel element
- [ ] Prices in mono only
- [ ] Hours format identical: `Wed–Fri 8–5 · Sat–Sun 10–5`
- [ ] QR URL matches `02_PHYSICAL_DISTRIBUTION_SYSTEM.md` table
