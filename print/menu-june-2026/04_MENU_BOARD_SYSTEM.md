# Menu Board System — Layout & Background

**June 2026** · In-venue column boards + print texture source

---

## Background treatment (decision)

**Yes — darken slightly.** Not pure black. Volcanic stone.

Take existing `menu-background.jpg` and:

| Adjustment | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Overall value | Darken **10–15%** |
| Texture contrast | Reduce **10–20%** |
| Color | Slightly **warm** (not cool gray) |

**Goal:** Visual silence — food and type float forward. Background disappears.

**Reference feeling:** Museum placard · boutique hotel signage — not generic café menu.

The mockup (`column-boards-render.png`) is closer to target than current single-column board.

## Two-column middle board (decision: yes)

**Not because it looks more "professional."** Because of how people scan at the door, in line, behind someone else.

| Left column | Right column |
|-------------|--------------|
| Food | Drinks |
| Erik's Addiction | Coffee |
| PB Bowl | Matcha |
| Toasts | Cold Brew |
| Açaí | Sides |

**Cognitive job:** Food | Drinks — divider does scanning work.

**Copy rule:** Two-column structure + **less text than AI mockup**. Not restaurant-menu syndrome.

```
ERIK'S ADDICTION
almond butter · banana · cacao nibs
11
```

Not five lines of ingredients. Hideout menu is small — use that advantage.

**Typography:** Slightly larger than current single-column. More negative space.

**Feeling:** House guide, not inventory list.

> "Here's how to navigate the house" — not "here's everything we sell."

---

## Single-column when to keep (everything else)

Placement cards, lobby stand, Sunday exit, Watermarc, counter — **always single column.**

Single-column is editorial and premium — the house speaking. Correct for orientation. Wrong default for **standing scan** at peak window (middle board only).

Do not port Food | Drinks layout to placement cards. Restaurant-menu aesthetics are not Hideout.
