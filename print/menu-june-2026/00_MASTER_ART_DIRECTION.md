- Boutique hotel room-service card

**Feeling:** Calm competence.

The piece should feel like **someone thoughtful put this here** — not **someone is trying to get my attention**.

---

## One design language, six placements

Do **not** make SkyView, Sunday exit, and office cards look like different brands.

**Same on every piece:**
- Void black background (`#0E0C0A` family)
- Cream typography (`#F5ECD7`)
- **One** caramel accent (`#C8852A`) — QR frame or single rule line
- Same margins, grid, QR treatment
- `menu-background.jpg` texture + void multiply overlay

**Only the message changes. The system stays.**

Reference: existing column boards in this folder (`column-boards-render.png`) — dark, cream type, no decoration. Cards are compressed versions of that language.
