## Settings (all tools)

- **Resolution:** 2048×2048 minimum for print backgrounds
- **Style:** Photographic — not illustrated
- **Avoid:** HDR, AI glow, oversaturation, obvious compositing
- **Palette:** Dark void, warm highlights, cream tones, film grain

## Post-generation checklist

Before importing to Photoshop:

- [ ] No people, faces, or human subjects
- [ ] No unintended text or signage
- [ ] No third-party brand logos
- [ ] Dark enough for cream type overlay
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio for placement
- [ ] Saved PNG or high-quality JPG
